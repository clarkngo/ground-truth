# Merit Scholarships

## Larkspur Merit Scholarship
Incoming undergraduates with a high school or transfer GPA of 3.5 or higher are considered for the Larkspur Merit
Scholarship of $2,000 per academic year.

## Renewal
The scholarship renews each year if the student keeps a cumulative GPA of at least 3.2 and completes at least 36
credits during the academic year.

## Deadline
Applications are due March 1 for students starting the following Fall.
