# Course Catalog: Artificial Intelligence

All AI courses are 5 credits unless noted.

## AI 210 Foundations of Machine Learning
Supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation and bias. Includes a lab.
Prerequisites: CS 160 and MATH 201. Offered: Fall, Winter.

## AI 220 Data Engineering for AI
Data pipelines, feature stores and data quality for machine learning. Includes a lab.
Prerequisite: CS 160. Offered: Fall, Spring.

## AI 330 Deep Learning
Neural networks, optimization, CNNs and transformers. Includes a lab.
Prerequisites: AI 210 and MATH 220. Offered: Winter, Spring.

## AI 340 Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models
Text representations, transformers, retrieval-augmented generation and evaluation of language models. Includes a lab.
Prerequisite: AI 210. Offered: Spring.

## AI 350 Agentic Systems
Tool use, planning, multi-agent orchestration and agent safety. Includes a lab.
Prerequisite: AI 340. Offered: Fall.

## AI 360 Human-AI Interaction
Designing and evaluating AI products with people in mind. No lab.
Prerequisite: junior standing. Offered: Winter.

## AI 370 MLOps
Deploying, monitoring and maintaining models in production. Includes a lab.
Prerequisites: AI 210 and AI 220. Offered: Spring, Summer.

## AI 395 Internship
Supervised work experience in AI, 1 to 5 credits. See the Internships for Credit page.

## AI 410 AI Capstone
A two-quarter-equivalent team project delivered in one quarter with an industry or research partner. Includes a lab.
Prerequisites: AI 330, AI 340 and senior standing. Offered: Fall, Spring.
