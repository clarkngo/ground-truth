# Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)

Students receiving financial aid must meet three standards, reviewed at the end of every quarter.

## Standards
1. **GPA:** a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 for undergraduates and 3.0 for graduate students.
2. **Pace:** complete at least 67% of all attempted credits.
3. **Maximum timeframe:** finish the degree within 150% of its required credits. For a 180-credit bachelor's degree
   this is 270 attempted credits.

## Warning and suspension
A student who misses a standard is placed on Financial Aid Warning for one quarter. Missing a standard again leads to
Financial Aid Suspension.

## Appeals
Students on suspension may appeal with an academic plan approved by an advisor.
