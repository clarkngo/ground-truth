# Academic Calendar, 2025-26

Each quarter is numbered by week, starting with week 1 on the first day of instruction.

| Quarter | First day | Last day |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2025 | September 29, 2025 | December 12, 2025 |
| Winter 2026 | January 5, 2026 | March 20, 2026 |
| Spring 2026 | March 30, 2026 | June 12, 2026 |
| Summer 2026 | June 22, 2026 | August 28, 2026 |

Finals week is the last week of each quarter.
