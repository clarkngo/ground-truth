# Research Computing: the Kestrel GPU Cluster

## Who can use it
Students enrolled in an AI course with a lab, research assistants and faculty may use the Kestrel cluster.

## GPU quotas
- Students in AI lab courses: 40 GPU-hours per quarter
- Research assistants: 200 GPU-hours per quarter
Extra hours can be requested through the research computing form and must be approved by a faculty sponsor.

## Job limits
A single job may run for at most 12 hours of wall time.

## Storage
Each user has 100 GB of scratch storage. Files in scratch are deleted after 30 days without access.

## Data rules
Data containing personally identifiable information (PII) may not be stored on Kestrel without approval from the
Institutional Review Board.
