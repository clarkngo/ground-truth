# Academic Integrity Policy

## Violation levels
- **Level 1** (minor, first offense): resolved by the instructor, for example through resubmission or a grade
  reduction on the assignment. Recorded internally but not on the transcript.
- **Level 2** (serious or repeated): referred to the Academic Integrity Board. Outcomes can include failing the
  course. Recorded in the student's conduct file.
- **Level 3** (egregious, such as exam impersonation or a third violation): may result in suspension or expulsion.

## Reporting
Instructors must report a suspected violation within 10 business days of discovering it.

## Appeals
Students may appeal a finding within 15 business days of being notified.
