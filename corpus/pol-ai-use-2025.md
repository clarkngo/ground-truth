# Generative AI Use in Coursework

This policy takes effect in Fall 2025 and replaces the 2024 Generative AI Use in Coursework policy.

## Default rule
Generative AI tools are permitted on coursework by default, with disclosure, unless the course syllabus restricts
or prohibits them.

## Exams and proctored assessments
Generative AI is prohibited in exams and proctored assessments unless the exam instructions explicitly allow it.

## Disclosure
Every submission that used generative AI must include a disclosure statement that names the tool and describes how it
was used (for example: brainstorming, drafting, debugging or editing).

## Theses and capstones
AI tools may not be listed as authors or co-authors of theses or capstone reports.

## Violations
Undisclosed AI use is a Level 2 academic integrity violation. Use of AI where the syllabus prohibits it is also a
Level 2 violation.
