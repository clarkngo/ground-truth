# Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

## Overview
The B.S. in Computer Science requires 180 credits.

## Required core courses
- CS 160 Programming in Python
- CS 210 Data Structures
- CS 230 Computer Systems
- CS 310 Algorithms
- CS 340 Databases
- CS 450 Computer Science Capstone
- MATH 201 Discrete Mathematics and Probability

## Electives
Students complete 25 credits of CS or AI electives numbered 300 or above.
