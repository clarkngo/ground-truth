# Registration, Add/Drop and Withdrawal

## Registration windows
Registration for the next quarter opens in week 7 of the current quarter. Students register in priority order based
on credits earned, with graduate students and seniors first.

## Adding courses
Courses may be added through the end of week 1 of the quarter. Adding a course in week 2 requires instructor
permission.

## Dropping courses
Courses dropped by the end of week 2 do not appear on the transcript.

## Withdrawing from courses
From week 3 through week 7, students may withdraw from a course and receive a grade of W, which does not affect GPA.
After week 7, withdrawal is only possible through a medical or emergency petition to the Dean of Students.
For refunds that apply when dropping or withdrawing, see the Tuition Refund Schedule.
