# Master of Science in Data Science

## Overview
The M.S. in Data Science requires 45 credits. Full-time students taking 9 credits per quarter finish in 5 quarters.

## Admission
Applicants need a bachelor's degree, a completed statistics course and working knowledge of Python.

## Required courses
- DS 510 Statistical Learning
- DS 520 Applied Machine Learning
- DS 530 Data Visualization and Communication
- DS 590 Data Science Capstone

The remaining credits are electives from DS or AI courses numbered 500 or above.
