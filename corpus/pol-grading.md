# Grading Policy

## Letter grades and grade points
Larkspur Institute of Technology uses the following scale: A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7,
C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C- = 1.7, D = 1.0, F = 0.0. Grade point average (GPA) is calculated from graded credits only.

## Minimum grades for major courses
A course counts toward a student's major only with a grade of C (2.0) or higher. A C- or lower in a major course
must be repeated. When a course is repeated, only the most recent grade counts toward the GPA, and a course may be
attempted at most three times.

## Incomplete grades
An instructor may assign an Incomplete (I) when a student has completed at least 70% of the coursework but cannot
finish for documented reasons. The remaining work must be completed within two quarters; otherwise the Incomplete
converts to an F.

## Pass/No Pass
Undergraduates may count at most 12 credits of Pass/No Pass coursework toward a degree. Courses required for the
major cannot be taken Pass/No Pass. A grade of Pass requires work equivalent to a C or better.
