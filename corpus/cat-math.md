# Course Catalog: Mathematics

All MATH courses are 5 credits.

## MATH 110 Precalculus
Functions, exponentials, logarithms and trigonometry. Offered every quarter.

## MATH 201 Discrete Mathematics and Probability
Logic, sets, combinatorics and probability. Prerequisite: MATH 110 or placement. Offered every quarter.

## MATH 220 Linear Algebra
Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues and applications to data. Prerequisite: MATH 201. Offered: Fall, Winter, Spring.
