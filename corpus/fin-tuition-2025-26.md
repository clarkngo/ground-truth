# Tuition and Fees, 2025-26 Academic Year

These rates take effect in Fall 2025 and replace the 2024-25 rates.

## Tuition
- Undergraduate: $435 per credit
- Graduate: $665 per credit

## Fees
- Technology fee: $110 per quarter for every enrolled student
- Lab fee: $75 per lab course
- GPU compute fee: $40 per quarter for students enrolled in at least one AI course with a lab
