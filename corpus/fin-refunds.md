# Tuition Refund Schedule

Refunds apply to tuition when a student drops or withdraws from a course.

| When the course is dropped | Tuition refunded |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1-2 | 100% |
| Week 3 | 75% |
| Week 4 | 50% |
| Week 5 | 25% |
| Week 6 or later | 0% |

Fees (technology, lab and GPU compute fees) are refundable only through week 2.
