# Academic Advising

## First-year students
First-year students must meet with their academic advisor every quarter before registering. Students who skip the
meeting have a registration hold placed on their account.

## Continuing students
Continuing students are encouraged to meet with an advisor at least once per academic year and must meet with one
before applying to graduate.
