# Student IT Accounts and Security

## Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all student accounts. Students must use an authenticator app or a
hardware security key. Text-message (SMS) codes are no longer accepted as of Fall 2025.

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Regular password rotation is not required, but a password must be changed
immediately if it may have been exposed.

## Account lifecycle
Student accounts are deactivated 180 days after the student's last enrolled quarter. Email is retained for one year
after deactivation and then deleted.
