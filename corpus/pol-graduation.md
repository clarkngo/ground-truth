# Graduation Requirements and Honors

## Applying to graduate
Students must apply for graduation by the end of week 3 of the quarter before their final quarter.

## Requirements
- A cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 (3.0 for graduate degrees).
- Residency: the last 45 credits of a bachelor's degree must be completed at Larkspur.

## Latin honors (bachelor's degrees)
- Cum laude: cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher
- Magna cum laude: 3.7 or higher
- Summa cum laude: 3.9 or higher
