# Library Hours and Study Rooms

## Regular hours during the quarter
- Monday to Thursday: 7 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- Friday: 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- Saturday: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- Sunday: noon to 10 p.m.

## Finals week
During finals week the library is open 24 hours from Sunday through Thursday.

## Study rooms
Group study rooms hold up to 6 people and can be booked for up to 2 hours per day per student.
