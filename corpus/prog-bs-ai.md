# Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence

## Overview
The B.S. in Artificial Intelligence requires 180 credits. Students learn machine learning, language models, agentic
systems and MLOps, with a capstone project in the final year.

## Required core courses
- CS 160 Programming in Python
- MATH 201 Discrete Mathematics and Probability
- MATH 220 Linear Algebra
- AI 210 Foundations of Machine Learning
- AI 220 Data Engineering for AI
- AI 330 Deep Learning
- AI 340 Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models
- AI 410 AI Capstone

## Electives
Students complete 20 credits of electives from AI courses numbered 300 or above, excluding the core.

## Math readiness
Students start in MATH 201 if they place into it or have completed MATH 110 with a grade of B or higher. Otherwise they
take MATH 110 first.
