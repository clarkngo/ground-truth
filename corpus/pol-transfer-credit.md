# Transfer Credit Policy

## Limits
Up to 90 transfer credits may count toward a 180-credit bachelor's degree. Graduate programs accept up to 10
transfer credits.

## Eligibility
Transferred courses must come from a regionally accredited institution with a grade of C or better.
Technical major courses completed more than 10 years ago require a review by the program chair.

## Exams for credit
Advanced Placement (AP) scores of 4 or 5 earn credit. International Baccalaureate higher-level scores of 5 or above
earn credit.
