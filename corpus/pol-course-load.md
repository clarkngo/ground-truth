# Course Load Policy

## Undergraduate
Full-time undergraduate enrollment is 12 to 18 credits per quarter. Enrolling in more than 18 credits requires advisor
approval and a cumulative GPA of at least 3.3.

## Graduate
Full-time graduate enrollment is 9 or more credits per quarter.
