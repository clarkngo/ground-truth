# Generative AI Use in Coursework

This policy applies from the 2024-25 academic year.

## Default rule
Use of generative AI tools on graded work is prohibited unless the instructor explicitly permits it in writing.

## When AI use is permitted
If an instructor permits AI use, students must include a disclosure statement with the submission naming the tool.

## Violations
Unauthorized AI use is treated as an academic integrity violation and handled under the Academic Integrity Policy.
