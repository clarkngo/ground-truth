# Internships for Credit (AI 395 and CS 395)

## Eligibility
Students may earn internship credit after completing at least 90 credits with a cumulative GPA of 2.5 or higher.

## Credit
Internships earn 1 to 5 credits. Each credit requires 30 hours of supervised work. At most 10 internship credits count
toward a bachelor's degree.

## Approval
The internship must be approved by a faculty supervisor before work begins.
